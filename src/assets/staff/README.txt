STAFF PHOTOS
Put a portrait photo in this folder and it appears automatically on the Leadership, Staff and Directory pages.

Name the file after the person, in lower case with hyphens, and keep the extension .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp:

  Dr. Joshua Kato Kimata         ->  dr-joshua-kato-kimata.jpg
  Rt. Rev. James William Ssebaggala -> rt-rev-james-william-ssebaggala.jpg
  Ms. Grace Akello               ->  ms-grace-akello.jpg

Use square-ish photos, at least 300 x 300 px, face centred. People without a photo keep their initials.
